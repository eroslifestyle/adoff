Ad Blocker for Italian Catch-Up TV: RaiPlay, Infinity, La7

AdOff removes pre-roll and mid-roll ads from Italian catch-up TV platforms in the browser, in most cases. It swaps the Google IMA ad SDK those players request for a local stub that instantly resumes the programme, so you watch RaiPlay, Infinity or La7 without interruptions.

Italian services covered

Italian broadcasters monetise their free catch-up platforms with video advertising, and their players rely on the standard Google IMA SDK in the browser. AdOff covers:

  • RaiPlay (raiplay.it, rai.it)
  • Mediaset Infinity (mediasetinfinity.mediaset.it, mediaset.it)
  • La7 (la7.it)
  • Discovery+ (discoveryplus.com)

The mechanism, step by step

When a RaiPlay or Infinity page loads in your browser, the player normally downloads the IMA SDK from Google's servers to schedule ad breaks. AdOff redirects that request to a stub bundled with the extension. The stub speaks enough of the SDK's language to keep the player happy, and when the player asks to start an ad it answers "content resume requested" straight away.

The programme then plays without the break. No video is re-downloaded and nothing about the content stream changes.

Why this works on Italian public and commercial broadcasters

Rai, Mediaset and Discovery monetise catch-up viewing with ads inserted through a standard SDK, which is exactly the layer a browser extension can intercept. Subscription platforms like Prime Video instead stitch ads into the stream server-side, and those are deliberately left out of the extension so their players keep working. Catch-up TV is the segment where the technical approach has real traction.

Network and cosmetic filtering on top

Beyond the player, AdOff ships 190 static network rules blocking ad and tracking domains, plus a CSS/DOM filter that hides banners and promotional overlays on the broadcaster's pages. On RaiPlay or Infinity this means fewer promo tiles and billboards around the catalogue, not just fewer video interruptions.

What it cannot do

Be realistic about the edges:

  • If a platform adopts server-side ad insertion (SSAI), the ad becomes part of the video stream and the stub no longer applies.
  • It works in the desktop browser only: RaiPlay smart TV apps, mobile apps and casting are out of scope.
  • Some live events and sport use different delivery pipelines.
  • Players change often; coverage is actively maintained but never frozen.

Frequently asked questions

Does AdOff remove ads from RaiPlay?

In most cases yes, when the RaiPlay web player loads its ads through the Google IMA SDK. The extension replaces that SDK with a stub that immediately resumes the programme. Where Rai experiments with other insertion techniques, coverage can vary.

Can I skip the ads on Mediaset Infinity for free?

AdOff is a free browser extension that neutralises the ad SDK in the Infinity web player, so pre-rolls and mid-rolls usually do not play. It does not touch your Infinity account and offers no paid tier — every feature is included.

What about La7 and Discovery+?

Both are covered the same way: the IMA SDK request is redirected to a local stub and the player resumes content instead of running the break. This applies to the desktop browser versions of the services.

Does this involve the Rai licence fee (canone)?

No. AdOff only affects advertising inside the player and on the page. Questions about the canone di abbonamento are entirely separate and unrelated to this extension.

Does it work on the RaiPlay app or smart TV?

No. AdOff is a browser extension for desktop browsers. Mobile apps, smart TV apps and streaming sticks are outside its reach.
