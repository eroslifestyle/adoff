How AdOff works on Android

🦊

Firefox for Android

Unlike Chrome, Firefox for Android supports add-ons. That's the one browser on Android where a real extension-based ad blocker can run.

🧩

Add the AdOff extension

Open Firefox's Add-ons menu, search for AdOff and add it. Two minutes, no account, nothing to sideload.

🛡️

Network + cosmetic blocking

AdOff blocks ad and tracker requests at the network level and hides ad slots in the page — the same filtering as on desktop.

🎬

Video ad neutralization

The same video ad neutralization as desktop works in Firefox for Android too: pre-roll video ads are skipped with no countdown, and the player keeps working.

Set it up in 3 steps

Works wherever Firefox for Android runs

  1. Install Firefox for Android

    Get it from the Google Play Store (or your usual app store). It's free and takes a minute.

  2. Add the AdOff extension

    In Firefox, open the menu → Add-ons, search for AdOff and tap Add. No account, no setup file.

  3. Browse ad-free

    Open any site in Firefox: ad and tracker requests are blocked, ad slots are hidden and video ads are neutralized — just like on desktop.
